What is Quizzy?

-Quizzy is an educational website that helps students practice their knowledge through quizzes.

How do I start a quiz?

-Go to Quizzy, select your semester and subject, then click Start Quiz.

What is Quizzy Checker?

-Quizzy Checker allows users to enter questions, answer keys, and student answers to check results.

Can I upload a questionnaire image?

-Yes, You can upload an image and use the OCR feature to recognize its text.

Is Quizzy free to use?

-Quizzy is intended to provide accessible learning and practice activities for students.
